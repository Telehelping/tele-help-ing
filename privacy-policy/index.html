LEGAL & PRIVACY

Your information.
Handled responsibly.

What we collect, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tele-Help-Ing, Inc. ("Tele-Help-Ing," "we," "us") handles information when you visit telehelping.com, communicate with us, use our optional SMS consent chat, or interact with our services.

This site serves as the main website for the Tele-Help-Ing group. Some linked brands maintain separate websites and may have their own privacy policies.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, this may include your name, company, email address, phone number, information in messages you send us, business service needs, correspondence, and records of your consent preferences.

When you use our optional HighLevel chat widget, we may collect information you submit and records relating to your messaging preferences and consent.

Our website and service providers may also process technical information, including IP addresses, browser information, device details, and usage data.

2. How We Use Information

  • Respond to inquiries and provide requested information.
  • Support client relationships and business communications.
  • Coordinate staffing services, onboarding, and scheduling.
  • Send SMS messages where appropriate consent has been obtained.
  • Maintain consent records and honor opt-out requests.
  • Maintain website performance, security, and legal compliance.

3. SMS Consent and Mobile Information

Providing your phone number does not automatically enroll you in SMS communications.

Before sending text messages, we obtain consent appropriate to the type of communication. Promotional messaging requires separate consent where applicable.

Consent to receive marketing text messages is not a condition of purchasing services.

You may reply STOP to cancel SMS messages or HELP for assistance. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We may use service providers to support our communications on our behalf. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional use.

SMS consent provided to Tele-Help-Ing does not automatically authorize separate messaging programs operated by other brands.

4. Service Providers and Information Sharing

We use service providers for website hosting, communications, customer relationship management, and technical infrastructure. These providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to provide services.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law. We do not sell mobile SMS consent information or phone numbers for marketing.

5. Links to Other Websites

Our website may link to other websites, including specialized brands within the Tele-Help-Ing group. Those websites may maintain separate privacy notices and information practices.

6. Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including service delivery, consent records, business records, and legal obligations.

We use administrative and technical safeguards intended to protect information. However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. International Processing

Tele-Help-Ing operates with team members and service providers in the United States and Mexico. Information may be accessed or processed in those locations as needed for business operations, subject to applicable protections.

8. Your Privacy Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of applicable personal information by contacting info@telehelping.com.

We evaluate privacy requests under applicable law and may retain certain records when legally required. You can opt out of text messages by replying STOP.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this policy as our business practices, technology, or legal requirements change. The date above indicates the latest revision.
