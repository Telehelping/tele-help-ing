FAQ

Clear expectations before you book.

Does this call promise a completed hire?

No. The discovery call helps clarify needs and next steps. A completed hire depends on client participation, candidate availability, final selection, and applicable agreement terms.

Do you promise candidate performance?

No. Tele-Help-Ing supports recruiting, screening, and candidate presentation. Candidate performance is not promised.

What happens after the call?

If there is a fit, the next step is to clarify or approve the Job Description and begin preparing the candidate search process under the applicable terms.
