The Tele-Help-Ing Placement Standard

Our Placement Commitment.

Tele-Help-Ing supports clients with a structured, AI-assisted recruiting process designed to source, screen, and present qualified candidates.

Our work is guided by commercially reasonable recruiting efforts, an approved Job Description, timely client participation, and the terms of the MSA.

Book a Discovery Call -> Review the search window

Important scope

Recruiting support with clear boundaries.

This placement standard explains how Tele-Help-Ing supports the candidate search and presentation process. It does not promise candidate performance, a completed hire, employment success, or business results.

The Initial Setup Fee is non-refundable. Any remedies or next steps apply only as stated in the MSA.

Commercially reasonable recruiting efforts
Qualified candidate sourcing and screening
Client participation and feedback required
MSA terms control scope and remedies

Qualified candidate criteria

What “qualified candidate” means in the recruiting process.

A qualified candidate is evaluated against the approved role profile and the practical needs of the position.

Skills

Relevant capabilities for the role, tools, workflows, and responsibilities described in the Job Description.

Experience

Prior background or task familiarity that supports the client’s role requirements.

Communication

Professional communication fit for remote work, client interaction, and day-to-day coordination.

Availability

Availability that aligns with the role expectations and agreed working schedule.

Role Alignment

Overall fit with the approved Job Description, required responsibilities, and selection criteria.

Candidate search window

The 60-day candidate search window.

After the Job Description is approved, Tele-Help-Ing uses commercially reasonable efforts to recruit, screen, and present qualified candidates within a 60-day search window.

This search window is a recruiting process standard. It does not promise a completed hire, candidate performance, or commercial outcomes.

60

Candidate search window after Job Description approval, subject to the MSA and timely client participation.

Applicability

When this commitment applies.

Approved Job Description

The role profile, responsibilities, and candidate criteria are approved before the search begins.

Timely Feedback

The client provides practical feedback on candidates, role fit, and interview outcomes within reasonable timelines.

Interview Participation

The client participates in interviews, evaluation, and next-step coordination as needed.

Timely Decisions

Hiring decisions and follow-up steps are made within reasonable timeframes so the search can progress.

Limitations

When this commitment may not apply.

The candidate search commitment may become unavailable if client-side changes or delays prevent the recruiting process from moving forward under the approved role profile.

Client delays in feedback, interviews, or decision-making
Paused recruiting activity or unavailability during the search
Material changes to the approved Job Description or role requirements
Rejection of three or more qualified candidates presented for the approved role

FAQ

Clear answers before your search begins.

Is candidate performance promised?

No. Tele-Help-Ing supports recruiting, screening, and candidate presentation. Candidate performance depends on many factors outside the recruiting process.

Is a completed hire promised?

No. Tele-Help-Ing makes commercially reasonable recruiting efforts and presents qualified candidates, but a completed hire depends on client participation, candidate availability, final selection, and MSA terms.

Are business results promised?

No. Tele-Help-Ing helps clients find and evaluate qualified remote talent. Business outcomes are not promised.

Is the Initial Setup Fee refundable?

No. The Initial Setup Fee is non-refundable.

What happens if no qualified candidate is presented within the search window?

Next steps are handled according to the MSA. The MSA controls the available remedies, conditions, and process for any continued recruiting support.
