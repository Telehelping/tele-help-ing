LEGAL & PRIVACY

Your information.
Handled responsibly.

What we collect, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tele-Help-Ing, Inc. ("Tele-Help-Ing," "we," "us") handles information when you visit telehelping.com, communicate with us, use our optional SMS consent chat, or interact with our services. This site serves as the main website for the Tele-Help-Ing group. Some linked brands maintain separate websites and may have their own privacy policies.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, this can include your name, company, email address, phone number, information in messages you send us, business service needs, correspondence, and records of your consent preferences. When you use the optional HighLevel chat widget, we may record the information you enter, the time of your submission, your checkbox selections, and related technical records necessary to document SMS consent. Our website and service providers may also process technical usage data such as IP address, browser information, and device details.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries, provide information you've requested, and support client or business relationships.
  • To coordinate staffing services, onboarding, scheduling, and operational communications where applicable.
  • To provide text messages of the type you separately agreed to receive, maintain proof of consent, and honor opt-out requests.
  • To maintain website performance, security, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

3. SMS, consent, and mobile information

Providing your phone number does not automatically enroll you in SMS messages. Where we seek SMS consent, it is requested through an optional, unchecked choice that explains the types of messages involved. Marketing and non-marketing message consent are treated separately where applicable. Consent to receive marketing text messages is not a condition of purchase. You may reply STOP to cancel SMS messages or HELP for assistance. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. See our Terms & SMS Conditions.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We may use service providers or subcontractors to support our communications and customer service on our behalf. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional use.

SMS consent for Tele-Help-Ing is not automatically transferred to a separate brand or business. A related business must obtain and honor consent appropriate to its own messaging program.

4. Service providers and disclosures

We use service providers to support operations such as website hosting, communications, CRM, and technical infrastructure. These providers may process information as needed to perform services for us, subject to appropriate contractual and legal controls. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or to protect our rights or users. We do not sell mobile SMS consent information or phone numbers for marketing.

5. Links to separate brands and websites

Our website may link to separately operated sites, including The Painter's VA. Clicking a link takes you to a different site. Review the privacy notices presented there; their practices may differ from ours.

6. Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, including service delivery, consent records, recordkeeping, and legal obligations. We use administrative and technical safeguards intended to protect information, but no Internet-based system is completely secure.

7. International processing

Tele-Help-Ing operates with team members and service providers in the United States and Mexico. Information may be accessed or processed in those locations as needed for our business operations, subject to applicable protections.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of applicable personal information by writing to info@telehelping.com. We will evaluate requests based on applicable law and our obligations to retain certain records. You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP.

9. Policy updates

We may update this policy when business practices, service providers, or legal obligations change. The date above indicates the last update. Material changes may be communicated through appropriate channels.
